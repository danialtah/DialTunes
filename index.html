Before you start spinning,

As of right now, Spotify's Developer API limits only allow a small handful of accounts (just my own personal account right now) to log in and access their library. Unfortunately that means you cannot connect your own personal account. If these limits change in the future, the code will be updated accordingly to let my original vision of anyone connecting their own account to work as intended!

To test out the functionality of the app, try the Demo!

Notable Demo limits

  • Short song previews (only ~20–30s per track).
  • No skipping forwards or backwards, Spotify doesn't support that for this kind of playback.
  • The available music is a small hand-picked catalog of tracks I have been enjoying recently!
